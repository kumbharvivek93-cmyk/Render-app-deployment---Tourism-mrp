Consumer Protection Support

Submit retailer complaints through a clear and official-looking public portal.

Tourist Sakha helps users report shops or retailers who force sales above the Maximum Retail Price. Register your account, sign in, and provide the incident details for complaint review.

Login to Continue Create New Account

Official Notice

This interface is intended for complaint intake. Please keep retailer contact details, location details, and event description ready before submission.

Before You Start

Use accurate information so the complaint can be reviewed without delay.

  • Register with your name, mobile number, email, and password.
  • Log in using your registered mobile number and password.
  • Submit retailer name, mobile number, address, and incident description.

Complaint Process

  1. Create your citizen account using the registration page.
  2. Access the complaint form through the login page.
  3. Enter retailer details and describe the over-MRP incident clearly.

Portal Purpose

The portal is designed to make complaint filing simple, readable, and trustworthy for public users. The interface follows a restrained government-style layout rather than a bright commercial design.
